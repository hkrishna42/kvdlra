Weeks 18–19 · the fair baseline · the official anchor · three families

Where the claim narrowed, and what survived it

The Week-18 panel's fatal gap was a missing 2-bit quantization baseline. Week 19 ran it faithfully (per-channel keys, per-token values, paired needle-for-needle) and an official NVIDIA RULER anchor. At matched stored bytes 2-bit KIVI ties the flagship on single needles and wins fluency; the flagship's edge is multi-value retrieval on our generator, which the official suite does not reproduce. What no quantizer reaches is the composed cell at 0.048× stored (Llama, Mistral) with full single/multi-key/multi-value retrieval — at a fluency cost, and not on Qwen.
