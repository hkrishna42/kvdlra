The KV cache dominates memory in long-context LLM inference. Most low-rank KV compressors fix their subspace at calibration or in one prefill pass; OjaKV opened the online, per-sequence axis with Oja's rule. We track it with the rank-adaptive BUG integrator for dynamical low-rank approximation (DLRA), whose error bound is independent of the smallest tracked singular value; swapping the tracker for Oja's rule or Frequent Directions in the same cache destroys retrieval or fails numerically. We pair this low-rank gist with a small surprise-selected exact tier and a warm-up seed. On Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen2.5-7B and Mistral-7B-v0.3 one configuration reaches perfect single- and multi-value 16K retrieval with no detected loss (12/12) at 0.15-0.28x honest stored state, 1.8-5x under ThinK and Palu; at 32K on Llama it beats ThinK on variable tracking (paired McNemar p=0.002) at 0.284x. Against a KIVI-scheme 2-bit baseline at matched bytes it wins multi-value retrieval under chunked prefill (at single-shot prefill the edge is not separated) but trails or ties on fluency; 4-bit quantization at twice the bytes is not separated from it; on the official NVIDIA RULER suite the two are not separated while eviction collapses. The two axes compose: the gist with 4-bit coordinates reaches 0.048x (16K) and 0.034x (32K) with full single/multi-key retrieval on Llama and Mistral on our generator, a depth no fixed-bit quantizer or eviction x quantization composite reaches, at a fluency cost, not on Qwen, and not yet on the official anchor, where the cell collapses. We map the limits (an r/n~0.25 retrieval wall, rank siphoning fixed by a singular-value floor, wide-KV variable tracking) and bill honestly: the ratio is stored state, not resident VRAM (measured decode residency 1.6x, decode 4-14x slower until a fused kernel); its payoff is a ~7x smaller persisted cache reloading 9-10x faster, shared with quantization.
