## What the method is (one paragraph)
**BUG** is a streaming KV-cache compressor for LLM inference. Per layer it maintains (1) a rank-`r`
low-rank "gist" of the retained K/V history, tracked online by the rank-adaptive BUG integrator
(Ceruti–Kusch–Lubich 2022, dynamical low-rank approximation,
`src/kvdlra/integrators/streaming_torch.py`), and (2) a small exact tier of `hh` verbatim tokens
selected by surprise = out-of-subspace residual (`src/kvdlra/cache/bug_cache.py`), plus attention
sinks, a recent ring, and a warm-up seed. Gist carries fluency, tier carries retrieval. Prefill is
chunked ingest; decode re-applies RoPE at true positions; attention runs on reconstructed K/V
(reconstruct-then-attend), so the headline ratio is *stored* state, not resident VRAM.
