Beyond the Two Walls: a mathematical attack plan for BUG

Weeks 1–10 didn't just produce results — they produced constraints. Two walls are measured, one diagnosis is confirmed, and every rival's edge is now mechanistically understood. This dashboard treats those as axioms and brainstorms what a proposal must look like to matter: each idea below names the wall it attacks, the mathematical mechanism, an adversarial pre-mortem, and a pre-registered falsifiable bar with a kill criterion. No idea is allowed to be un-killable.

8 proposals · 3 cheap probes runnable on CPU today 2 explicit "do not pursue" verdicts Every bar stated at honest matched memory

1 · The axioms: what 10 weeks proved (design constraints, not opinions)

Any new idea must be consistent with these measured facts — or explicitly explain which experimental condition it changes.

🧱 Axiom A — Tracking ceiling Wall 1

Streaming BUG is within ~0.7–1.4% of the Eckart–Young oracle in Frobenius norm, and beats FD (matched memory), iSVD, and Oja on real KV streams. FD needs 2× memory to tie.

Consequence: improving the tracker in the same metric is worth ≤1%. Gains must come from changing the objective, the manifold, or what is stored.

🧱 Axiom B — Overhead floor Wall 2

At ~89 tok-eq/layer, BUG's fixed state (basis 2nr + core + sinks + ring) is ~85% of budget; eviction pays ~zero. Codebook-on-coordinates (W8) could not escape it: the anchor is the overhead (~27% intrinsic fidelity floor). Chain of best levers closed only 60% of the gap (20.15 vs morph 18.71).

Consequence: extreme-budget wins require removing, sharing, or amortizing the basis itself — or making the tail cost zero.

🧱 Axiom C — Fidelity wall (rank vs context) Retrieval

A needle is a high-residual outlier, not a dominant direction: rank-r smears it. RULER@32K: BUG 0% at every rank; EA 100% at 0.10×. The wall crosses between 16K (r128 = 0.50) and 32K (0.00). W9: recovery works but required rank ∝ n (1.8× premium at ctx-2048 multi-key).

Consequence: retrieval needs an exactness channel orthogonal to rank — verbatim, coded, or recoverable.
